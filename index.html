Secure Disclosure Platform

A legal, supervised vulnerability disclosure system for governments, banks, and telecom operators in Africa. Researchers report through a protected channel. Every finding is triaged by a human before any action. No unauthorized access. No exploitation. Disclosure only.

How it works

  1. Researcher submits a report through the secure portal with evidence (screenshots, logs, reproduction steps).
  2. AI pre-screens for duplicates and severity, then routes to a human triage officer.
  3. The organization acknowledges within 72 hours and assigns a fix timeline.
  4. Researcher is credited publicly (or anonymously) and receives a bounty per the published scale.
