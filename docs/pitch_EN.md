# AICore Secure Disclosure — Pilot Proposal

**From:** Ely Cheikh Mourid, Founder & CEO, AICore Digital LLC, Richmond, Virginia
**To:** [Government Agency / Bank / Telecom]

## The problem
African governments and banks lose billions to cyber incidents, yet most have no legal channel for security researchers to report findings. Researchers go silent or sell to the highest bidder.

## The solution
A supervised vulnerability disclosure platform. Researchers submit reports with evidence. AI pre-screens for duplicates and severity. A human triage officer confirms every action. No unauthorized access. Disclosure only.

## Pilot
Two weeks. Read-only access to one system. If we surface one valid, previously unknown vulnerability, the pilot converts to a 12-month managed contract.
